About me

Hi! I’m Nguyen Song Thien Long (publishing as Long S. T. Nguyen ). I’m a final-year undergraduate student in Computer Science at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam.

I am currently a Research Assistant at the URA Research Group, working under the supervision of Prof. Quan Thanh Tho (Dean of the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, HCMUT). I am also an AI Researcher at the Center for AI Research (CAIR), VinUniversity, where I am mentored by Prof. Luu Anh Tuan (Nanyang Technological University, Singapore) and Dr. Nguyen Tran Cong Duy (Postdoctoral Researcher, CAIR). I also serve as a Principal Investigator at Saigon AI Hub, VNG Group.

Beyond research, I serve as a member of the Central Executive Committee of the Viet Nam National Union of Students for the 2023–2028 term, contributing to initiatives that promote scientific research, technology, and innovation among students across Vietnam.

My research interests lie in Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a focus on Information Retrieval (IR), Question Answering (QA), and Large Language Models (LLMs). My work centers on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), hybrid lexical–semantic retrieval, and reliable reasoning in both agent-based and standalone LLM systems. I am particularly interested in developing efficient, interpretable, and trustworthy methods for low-resource and multilingual settings, especially Vietnamese and under-resourced ethnic minority languages, with applications including Neural Machine Translation (NMT).
